## 3. Char Dham Yatra

The Char Dham circuit of Uttarakhand includes Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath, and Badrinath, and it is one of the most revered pilgrimage routes in India. The route is traditionally completed in a clockwise order and remains one of the strongest pilgrimage products for serious spiritual travellers. [web:98][web:105][web:106][web:107]

### Main points
- **Yamunotri**: origin of the Yamuna.
- **Gangotri**: origin of the Ganga.
- **Kedarnath**: Shiva shrine and one of the 12 Jyotirlingas.
- **Badrinath**: Vishnu shrine and major Vaishnava dham. [web:98][web:105][web:107]

### Suggested content blocks
- Route map.
- Opening season.
- Travel duration.
- Fitness and altitude guidance.
- Helicopter and road options.
- Senior-friendly and family-friendly planning.
