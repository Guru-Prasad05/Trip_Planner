## Subpage 2.1 — Koraput Highlands

### Meta
- **Title:** Koraput Adventure Tour | Deomali Trek, Waterfalls & Tribal Village Experience
- **Description:** Scale Odisha's highest peak Deomali at 1,672m, witness Raniduduma Falls, and immerse in tribal village life in Koraput. Custom group trekking and adventure packages.
- **Slug:** `/nature-tours/koraput`

### Hero
- **Headline:** *"The Call of the Wild: Koraput Adventures"*
- **Visual:** Panoramic Duduma Falls waterfall aerial view, lush green forested gorge
- **Tagline:** Odisha's untamed frontier — where peaks pierce clouds and waterfalls thunder through ancient forests.

### Destination Overview
Koraput is where the Eastern Ghats rise dramatically to meet the sky. At 1,672 metres, **Deomali Peak** — Odisha's highest summit — offers a summit camping experience under one of India's most star-dense night skies. The Talamali tabletop plateau blooms with wildflowers post-monsoon and transforms into a frost-kissed highland in December.

**Raniduduma Falls** — Odisha's tallest waterfall — plunges through ancient jungle gorges accessible only through a forested trail most groups never discover. The **Duduma Falls** on the Machkund River is a thundering cascade of water, mist, and sound — perfect for dawn photography.

The Koraput highlands are home to the **Kondh, Paroja, and Gadaba tribes**, who have lived in harmony with these forests for millennia. Weekly **Haat markets** offer an irreplaceable window into a living culture.

### Experiences
- Summit camping on Deomali Peak (overnight, with guide and gear)
- Sunrise trek to Raniduduma — misty, remote, and unforgettable
- Tribal village immersion with evening bonfire and folk performance
- Photography at Duduma Falls at golden hour
- White-water experience on Machkund River
- Haat market walk with tribal artisan interaction

### Best Season
October to February

### Ideal Group Size
6–30 people

- **Description:** Experience jeep safaris, Barehipani waterfall trek, birdwatching, and luxury eco-camp stays inside Odisha's UNESCO Biosphere Reserve. Group bookings for Similipal safari.

### Hero
- **Headline:** *"Where Tigers Walk in Sal Forests"*
- **Visual:** Similipal leopard on tree branch / collage of tigers, falls, sal forest
- **Tagline:** A UNESCO Biosphere Reserve where the rarest wildlife and India's most dramatic waterfalls coexist.

### Destination Overview
Similipal is Odisha's crown jewel of biodiversity. A UNESCO Biosphere Reserve and Project Tiger sanctuary, the park sprawls across 2,750 sq km of sal forests, grasslands, and river valleys. **Barehipani Falls (399m)** and **Joranda Falls (181m)** are among the most dramatic waterfalls in all of South Asia.

Jeep safaris reveal **Royal Bengal tigers, leopards, elephants, gaur, sambar, and the ultra-rare melanistic (black) leopard** — one of only a handful of wild melanistic big cat populations ever documented globally.

### Experiences
- Full-day and half-day jeep safari circuits
- Barehipani Falls trek (permit-required, 6-hour return)
- Birdwatching — 300+ species including hornbills and paradise flycatchers
- Luxury tented eco-camp inside the buffer zone
- Night sky photography (zero light pollution in buffer zones)
- Naturalist-led evening forest talks by campfire

### Best Season
November to April (safaris); Closed July–October

### Group Size
4–25 people | All permits pre-arranged

## Subpage 2.4 — Chilika Lake

### Meta
- **Title:** Chilika Lake Tour | Irrawaddy Dolphin Cruise, Migratory Birds & Houseboat Stay
- **Description:** Asia's largest brackish water lagoon — dawn dolphin cruises, Nalaban bird sanctuary, migratory birds from Siberia, and houseboat nights on Chilika. Group packages available.
- **Slug:** `/nature-tours/chilika`

### Hero
- **Headline:** *"Asia's Largest Lagoon — Magic Made Liquid"*
- **Visual:** Irrawaddy dolphin leaping from Chilika waters / sunset boat silhouette on lagoon
- **Tagline:** Where Irrawaddy dolphins surface in the morning gold and a million migratory birds fill the winter sky.

### Destination Overview
Chilika stretches over 1,100 sq km across three districts — Asia's largest brackish water lagoon and one of the world's most important Ramsar Wetlands. Every winter, over one million migratory birds arrive from Siberia, Mongolia, and Central Asia.

The **Irrawaddy dolphins** of Chilika are globally rare — fewer than 3,000 survive worldwide. A dawn boat ride towards Satpada offers almost guaranteed sightings of these gentle, round-nosed dolphins surfacing in morning gold.

### Experiences
- Dawn dolphin cruise — early departure from Barkul or Satpada
- Nalaban Bird Sanctuary visit (special permit arranged)
- Sunset boat safari with local fishermen
- Fresh catch seafood lunch on the lake — prawns, crabs, tilapia
- Overnight houseboat stay on the lagoon (group-bookable)
- Winter migratory bird watching (Nov–March)
- Photography — golden hour on the lagoon

### Best Season
November to March for birds | Year-round for dolphins

## Subpage 2.5 — Coastal Odisha

### Meta
- **Title:** Coastal Odisha Tour | Chandrabhaga Beach, Olive Ridley Turtles & Gopalpur Sea
- **Description:** Explore Odisha's pristine coastlines — Chandrabhaga Blue Flag beach near Konark, Olive Ridley turtle nesting at Rushikulya, and the colonial charm of Gopalpur-on-Sea. Group coastal tours.
- **Slug:** `/nature-tours/coastal-odisha`

### Hero
- **Headline:** *"From Golden Sunsets to Nesting Sea Turtles"*
- **Visual:** Chandrabhaga beach at sunrise with Konark temple silhouette visible
- **Tagline:** Pristine coastlines, uncrowded shores, and one of nature's greatest spectacles — the Olive Ridley Arribada.

### Destination Overview
While Puri's beach is famous, Odisha's coastline hides gems most visitors never find. **Chandrabhaga Beach** near Konark is one of India's Blue Flag beaches — ASI-restricted dunes meeting the Bay of Bengal without a single hotel on the horizon. **Gopalpur-on-Sea** is a Victorian-era port town with crumbling colonial bungalows, a lighthouse, and an uncrowded beach ideal for morning walks and fresh lobster dinners.

**Rushikulya Beach** in Ganjam district is the seasonal home of the **Olive Ridley sea turtles** — one of the world's largest mass-nesting events (Arribada), where tens of thousands of turtles simultaneously emerge to nest. Witnessing this under a full moon is a once-in-a-lifetime experience.

### Experiences
- Sunrise walk at Chandrabhaga with Konark as backdrop
- Private beach bonfire dinner at Gopalpur
- Olive Ridley turtle nesting observation — Dec to March (guided, low-impact)
- Sea kayaking along the pristine Puri-Konark marine drive
- Village-style coastal cooking experience with a local family
- Lighthouse climb at Gopalpur for coastal panorama

### Best Season
October to March | Turtle nesting: December to March

## Subpage 2.6 — Bhitarkanika Mangroves

### Meta
- **Title:** Bhitarkanika National Park Tour | Saltwater Crocodile Safari, Mangrove Boat Ride & Birdwatching
- **Description:** Explore India's best mangrove ecosystem — saltwater crocodile safaris, heronry birdwatching, and village stays in Bhitarkanika National Park, Odisha. Group bookings welcome.
- **Slug:** `/nature-tours/bhitarkanika`

### Hero
- **Headline:** *"Mangroves, Crocodiles & the World's Rarest Birds"*
- **Visual:** Motorboat gliding through dense mangrove channel, crocodile sunbathing on mudbank
- **Tagline:** India's finest mangrove ecosystem — raw, wild, and strangely peaceful.

### Destination Overview
Bhitarkanika's 672 sq km national park on the Brahmani-Baitarani delta holds India's **largest saltwater crocodile population**, including a world-record 23-foot specimen. Boat rides through mangrove channels, with crocodiles just metres away, are among the most visceral wildlife encounters in eastern India.

The park also hosts nesting colonies of **black-crowned night herons, painted storks, open-billed storks, and kingfishers**. Nearby Gahirmatha Beach is the world's largest Olive Ridley sea turtle rookery.

### Experiences
- Motorboat safari through mangrove channels
- Saltwater crocodile observation from close range (safely)
- Village stay with local Odia fishing communities
- Gahirmatha nesting beach observation (seasonal)
- Birdwatching in the Bagagahana heronry
- Photography of mangrove root systems at dawn

### Best Season
October to March
